A clearing price of $100k to $150k is likely.

## The property

- 2-story **porch-front brick row, 1,512 sf, 4 bed / 1 bath**, built ~1920, 16 x 65 lot, full basement, hot-water/steam heat, no central air. North side of the 2000 block of Mifflin, between 20th and 21st. One of ~20 identical 1,512-1,620 sf porch-fronts on that side of the block.
- **Condition: OPA rates interior and exterior 4 (below average).** No permits ever. Was a licensed rental 2005-2015 and cited as an unlicensed rooming house in 2012; L&I cases for windows, floors, ceilings, tub, stove and electric were closed by 2015.
- **Exterior (Street View Apr 2025 and L&I notice 10/22/2026):** the projecting second-floor bay is clad in old asbestos-cement/insulbrick siding with holes; cornice and porch fascia need repair and paint. First floor has replacement windows, a newer door and glass-block basement windows. Budget $15k for the bay re-clad (with possible asbestos abatement), cornice and porch.

## Why it's being sold

- Hearon and Richley owned it from 1981 and refinanced five times. The open loan is a **$66,660 Wachovia refinance from July 2006**. It was sold in 2018 to a distressed-note trust (Truman 2016 SC6) and in 2021 to a re-performing-loan trust (Citigroup 2021-RP1), so it defaulted, was likely modified, and defaulted again.
- 2017: Hearon deeded his share to Overton (transfer tax paid, so not a spouse or child), with Richley and Overton as joint tenants with survivorship.
- Complaint Aug 2024 for $91,811. Service dragged for 18 months. A loan modification was offered in early 2026 but Overton didn't sign it or appear at the March 2026 conciliation. Default judgment **$124,727** (June 2026); writ July 2026; first sale listing Nov 3.
- **Equity remains:** as-is ~$190-215k against a ~$130k payoff, so ~$60-85k. That gives her reason to fight (petition, bankruptcy) or sell before the date. If it sells at sheriff sale above the judgment, she gets the surplus.

## Business plan

**Plan A: flip (recommended, only after the title check).**
1. Win at $65k to $95k on Nov 3. Wire 10% + 1.5% premium the next business day, 90% within 15 days.
2. Wait about 12 months for the deed. Fix or appeal the L&I exterior notice once the deed records.
3. **Possession:** Overton is low-income; offer cash for keys right after the deed ($7.5k to $12k plus moving help), with ejectment as the fallback. Budget 8.5 months and $10k from the deed. Check for roomers.
4. **Rehab ~$125k over ~3 months (medium, dated to modern):** new kitchen, add a second full bath (4/2), electrical panel and wiring as needed, boiler service or replacement plus central air or mini-splits, floors, paint, windows where original, re-clad the second-floor bay, repair cornice and porch (+$15k). If the rooming-house layout or wiring is bad, this becomes heavy (~$179k) and the deal only works below ~$50k.
5. **List at $385k to $395k**, against 2011 Mifflin and the Moore/McKean/Sigel sales; expect about $375k.

**Plan B: light rehab and rent or resell.** A cosmetic 4/1.5 (like 2019 Mifflin) supports a ~$130k bid on the model, but resale for a light job is closer to $355k and the margin depends on the interior being decent. Renting at $2,400 to $2,600 (HUD 4BR SAFMR $2,410; 2019 Mifflin asks $2,600 under Section 8) does not hit 12% on cost at any realistic bid.

**Plan C: wholesale.** Assigning to a flipper supports about $108k in the base case, a floor if possession drags.
