## B. INFERENCES (not directly stated in a source)
1. **The "recent unsold listing" is probably not Unit C.** No source shows a Unit C sale listing after April 2021.
   - Likely sources of the belief: (a) **2102 Christian #C**, a near-identical 3rd-floor 1BR with a private deck in the same condo association, listed since Aug 2026, cut $5k and still unsold; and/or (b) the Auction.com foreclosure listing (Jul 2026 date apparently postponed, re-listed 2026-10-20).
   - An off-MLS or Zillow-only (FSBO/rental) listing of Unit C cannot be ruled out, because Zillow could not be accessed.
2. **$1,325 vs $1,295:** the only documented Unit C rent is $1,295 (pre-2021). $1,325 may be a later renewal under the current owner, or a misremembering. It is unconfirmed.
3. **Deck:** the 2021 MLS listing calls it "PRIVATE DECK"; the MLS outdoor field says "Deck", not "Roof Deck".
   - "Enter on the second floor, up the steps" and building stories = 3 suggest the unit is on the top floor, reached from a 2nd-floor entry.
   - The 2102 #C twin's "120 sq ft private deck with a pergola just off the back of the unit", tagged "Roof Deck", suggests that both buildings' top-floor units have a rear deck on the lower roof of the back building.
   - Whether it is a limited common element or a unit element, and whether its use is exclusive, can only be confirmed from the condo declaration and plats (recorded with the Phila. Recorder of Deeds) or from the association.
4. **Condo fee:** the fee was $212/mo in Jan 2021. In 2024-2026 the same association's units show $394 (Unit A, 2024) and $281 (2102 #C, 2026).
   - Fees appear to be allocated by unit and have risen.
   - The current Unit C fee is probably about $250-$300/mo. This is an estimate, not a figure from any source.
   - Unpaid association dues/liens on a foreclosed unit are a known risk; check with the association.
5. **Association name:** recorded variously as "2102, 12 Christian St Condo Association" and "2102,2112 Christian Street Condominiums". It appears to be **one association covering both 2102 and 2112 Christian**: six units, consistent with the consecutive OPA numbers 888303716 / 888303722.
6. **Market rent for Unit C (480 sf, private deck, walk-up):** about **$1,450-$1,600/mo**.
   - This sits above the $1,421 Christian St 515 sf comp and below the $1,695-$1,725 elevator-building units on South St.
   - It is consistent with automated estimates of $1,440-$1,501.
