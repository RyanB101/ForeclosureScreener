## Heirs and whether they were properly named
- Defendant: Stephanie Masci, in her capacity as administratrix of the estate (served personally 1/22/2026 and 8/21/2026 at 2233 S Front St). "Administratrix" means letters of administration (no will, or no executor).
- Heirs at law under PA intestacy (20 Pa.C.S. 2102-2103): widow Marie Bowers (first $30k + half, since both children are hers) and the two children. None of them was named individually; no "unknown heirs" were named.
- Pa.R.C.P. 1144 lets a plaintiff name the personal representative of a deceased mortgagor, so suing the administratrix alone is normally enough, IF letters were actually granted to her.
- Philadelphia's Register of Wills has no free online index, so I could not confirm the letters. PhilaDox / city records show nothing recorded for the estate.
- The plaintiff's own Oct 20, 2026 motion to vacate the judgment and discontinue (see docket_260102651.md) is the strongest sign that something in the party structure or notice was defective (e.g., no letters actually issued, a missing heir, or a pre-foreclosure notice problem). The reason is in the 16-page motion ($4 to buy on fjdefile.phila.gov), which I did not purchase.
