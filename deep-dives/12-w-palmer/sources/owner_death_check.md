## Register of Wills / estate
Philadelphia's Register of Wills has NO public online estate index. Records are requested by mail or in person (Room 180 City Hall; $20 research fee; 215-686-6282, RowOnline@phila.gov) with full name and date of death.
PhilaDox shows NOTHING recorded under Prem after 2019 (no estate deed, no letters, no inheritance-tax lien), so there is no sign an estate was opened that touched real estate.
